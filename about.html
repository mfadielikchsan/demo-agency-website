About us

Every brand has a story worth a click.

De Klikers is a creative and influencer marketing agency built around people, ideas, and the moments that make an audience stop scrolling, tap, and care.

Our story

De Klikers began with a simple idea: the best marketing feels like something a friend would show you.

The name says it. A "klik" is that instant of connection when someone stops scrolling and taps. We design campaigns and creator partnerships to earn that moment, and to keep the trust that comes after it.

Clicks are easy. Connection is the work.

Vision

To become a trusted creative partner for brands and creators, building meaningful connections that start with a click and last long after it.

Mission
  • Create brand and creator collaborations that feel genuine, not scripted.
  • Deliver creative content that earns attention instead of buying it.
  • Build strategic campaigns measured by real engagement, not vanity clicks.
  • Grow a collaborative creator community where talent is developed, not just booked.
